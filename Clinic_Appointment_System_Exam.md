# Clinic Appointment System – Systems Analysis & Design Examination

## Scenario

A small medical clinic in Madang currently manages patient records and appointments manually using paper files and notebooks. Due to increasing patient numbers and difficulties managing records, the clinic plans to introduce a computerized Clinic Appointment System.

The new system will help clinic staff:

- Register patients
- Schedule appointments
- Record nurse assessments
- Store doctor consultation details electronically

### System Users

- Receptionists
- Nurses
- Doctors

---

# System Description

## 1. Patient Registration

When a new patient arrives at the clinic, the receptionist records the patient's details into the system.

### Information Recorded

- Patient ID
- Full Name
- Date of Birth
- Gender
- Phone Number

---

## 2. Appointment Scheduling

- Appointment ID
- Appointment Date
- Appointment Time
- Assigned Doctor

---

## 3. Nurse Assessment

- Temperature
- Blood Pressure
- Symptoms

---

## 4. Doctor Consultation

- Diagnosis
- Treatment Notes

---
